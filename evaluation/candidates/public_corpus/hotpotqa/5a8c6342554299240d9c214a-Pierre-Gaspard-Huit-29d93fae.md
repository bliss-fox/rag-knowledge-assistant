# Pierre Gaspard-Huit

Pierre Gaspard-Huit (29 November 1917 – 1 May 2017) was a French film director and screenwriter. He directed the 1963 film "Shéhérazade", which starred Anna Karina. He was once married to actress Claudine Auger when she was 18, and he was 43 years old. She acted in several of his films.
