# Franchesca Salcedo

Franchesca "Cruzita" Salcedo (born Franchesca Salcedo on March 16, 2002 in San Pablo City, Laguna, Philippines) is a Filipina child actress . She plays the title role of Cruzita Aldama Santibañez in "MariMar" daughter of Marimar Aldama and Sergio Santibañez. Although her nickname in real life is Cruzita, her acting roles in both "MariMar" and "Claudine" had her playing a character named Cruzita, which she was given after her stint on the former.
